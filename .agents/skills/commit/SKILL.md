# Commit

1. Before committing, inspect the complete staged and unstaged diff and account for every changed hunk by its reason.
2. Stage cohesive, reviewable commits by feature or concern. Separate configuration, tooling, refactors, and product behavior when their reasons differ.
3. Use Conventional Commits for every commit message.
